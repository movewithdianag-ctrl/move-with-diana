# How to edit your website — a guide for Diana

This guide explains how to change anything on movewithdianag.com yourself:
words, photos, offers, FAQs — everything. No technical knowledge needed.
Keep this somewhere handy.

One thing to know before you start: **you cannot break the website.** The
editor only lets you change words and photos — never the design or the way
the site works. And every change you make is saved as a snapshot, so anything
can be undone.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open your web browser (Safari or Chrome) and type
   **movewithdianag.com/admin** into the **address bar** — the long bar
   where website addresses go, at the very top of the window (on an iPhone
   it can sit at the bottom). Then press Enter / Go.
   ⚠️ Don't type it into the Google search box — this page is deliberately
   hidden from Google to keep it private, so searching finds nothing.
   (Once you're there, bookmark it — on an iPhone: tap the Share button,
   then "Add Bookmark".)
2. Click **Login**.
3. Sign in one of two ways:
   - **With Google** — click "Sign in with Google" and pick your
     movewithdianag@gmail.com account. That's it.
   - **With email and password** — the email is movewithdianag@gmail.com and
     the password is the one you chose when you accepted the invitation
     email. Forgot it? Click "Forgot password" and check your email.

You'll see a screen titled **"Website content"** with a list of sections:
Site settings, Home page, About page, Offers, Contact page, Footer & extras,
and Testimonials.

## 2. Changing words on the site

1. Click the section you want — for example **Home page**.
2. You'll see labelled boxes: "Big headline", "Sentence under the headline",
   and so on. Little grey notes under some boxes explain what they control.
3. Click into any box and type your change.
4. When you're happy, click the blue **Publish** button at the top, then
   **Publish now**.
5. **Your change appears on the live website about 2 minutes later.** (The
   site rebuilds itself — like a tiny printing press run.) Refresh the page
   after a couple of minutes to see it.

If you start editing and change your mind, just click the back arrow
(top-left) and choose to discard — nothing is saved until you press Publish.

## 3. Adding, removing, or reordering things in a list

FAQs, offers, dropdown options, the photo strip, and testimonials are
**lists**. Every list works the same way:

Every item in a list has a thin **grey bar across its top**. The small
arrow at the bar's left end folds the item open or closed; the small **x**
at its right end deletes it.

- **Add one:** click the **＋ Add** button under the list, then fill in the
  boxes.
- **Delete one:** click the small **x** at the right end of the item's grey
  bar. Careful — it deletes immediately, with no "are you sure?". Clicked it
  by mistake? Click the back arrow (top-left) and choose to discard —
  nothing is really gone until you press Publish.
- **Reorder:** click and hold the middle of the grey bar, then drag the item
  up or down.

**Example — adding a new testimonial:**

1. Click **Testimonials (saved for later)**.
2. Under "Client testimonials", click **＋ Add client testimonials**.
3. Fill in "Client name" and "Their words".
4. Click **Publish → Publish now**. Done.

## 4. Swapping a photo

1. Open the section the photo lives in (for example **About page** for your
   portrait, or **Offers** for the offer photos).
2. Find the photo box — it shows a small preview of the current photo.
3. Click **Choose different image**. A window full of photos opens.
4. Click **Upload**, and pick the new photo from your computer or phone.
5. When the upload finishes, your new photo appears in that window with a
   highlight around it. (No highlight? Click the photo once.)
6. Click **Choose selected** at the bottom of the window. The window closes
   and the photo box now shows your NEW photo. If it still shows the old
   one, the swap didn't take — do steps 3–6 again.
7. Click **Publish → Publish now**.

Tips for photos:

- Use photos straight from your camera/phone — the site automatically fits
  them into the right shape, so you don't need to crop anything.
- Tall (portrait) photos look best in most spots; the grey note under each
  photo box tells you if a wide one is better.
- Also update the "Photo description" box — it's a one-sentence description
  read aloud to visitors who use screen readers.

## 5. Changing the weekly schedule

The schedule doesn't live in this editor — it lives in your
**Google Calendar**, which you already know how to use:

- **Add a class:** open Google Calendar (on your phone is fine) and create
  the event — but before saving, do the ONE step that matters: on the event
  screen, tap the calendar name (it will show **your own name** — that's
  your personal calendar) and switch it to *In-Person — Move with Diana* or
  *Online — Move with Diana*. The website only shows those two calendars;
  events on your personal calendar never appear on it.
- **A class you added isn't showing on the site?** That's almost always it —
  open the event and check which calendar it's on.
- **Move or cancel a class:** open the event and change or delete it. The
  website updates itself — nothing to publish.
- **Cancel just one week of a repeating class:** open that day's event,
  delete it, and choose **"This event"** (not "All events").
- The schedule section is currently **hidden** on the website. To show it:
  go to **Site settings**, turn on **"Show the weekly schedule section"**,
  and Publish.

## 6. Other useful switches (in Site settings)

- **Community link (Skool):** this ONE box controls where ALL the community
  buttons on your website go — the top-right button, the big button on the
  home page, the "Join the community" buttons on the About and Offers pages,
  and the link in the footer. Change the address here once and every button
  follows automatically. (If you ever empty this box, the buttons open the
  contact form instead and the footer link simply hides itself — nothing
  breaks.)
- **Social media links:** update these if your Instagram/TikTok/YouTube
  addresses ever change.
- **Menu labels and the top-right button:** rename them any time. The menu
  links always lead to the same pages; the top-right button leads wherever
  the Community link box says.

**Worked example — updating your Skool link** (5 minutes, start to finish):

1. Go to **movewithdianag.com/admin** and log in (section 1).
2. Click **Site settings**.
3. Find the box labelled **Community link (Skool)**.
4. Click into it, delete the old address, and paste the new one — the whole
   thing, starting with `https://`. For example:
   `https://www.skool.com/move-with-diana-9111/about`
5. Click **Publish**, then **Publish now**.
6. Wait 2 minutes. Open **movewithdianag.com**, click the top-right button,
   and check it lands where you expect. Done.

## 7. If something looks wrong

Work through this list in order:

1. **Wait 2–3 minutes, then refresh.** Changes take about 2 minutes to go
   live. On a phone, close the tab and reopen it.
2. **Hard-refresh your browser** — sometimes it shows you an old saved copy.
   On a computer: hold **Shift** and click the reload button. On an iPhone:
   open Safari, tap the two-squares button in the bottom-right corner, tap
   the **"X Tabs"** button at the bottom and choose **Private**, tap **＋**,
   and type movewithdianag.com — a private tab always shows the true,
   current website.
3. **Undo your change.** Go back into the editor, fix the text or re-pick
   the old photo, and Publish again.
4. **Still wrong, or you're locked out?** Message Nick. Every change is
   snapshotted, so anything — anything — can be rolled back. You have not
   broken it.

## 9. How your website actually works (the four pieces)

You never need to touch any of this — but knowing the names stops it feeling
like magic, and it's here for anyone helping you:

1. **GitHub** — the filing cabinet. Every file your website is made of lives
   here, plus a saved copy of every version there has ever been. When you
   press Publish in the editor, your change is filed into this cabinet.
2. **Netlify** — the printing press. Seconds after anything lands in the
   cabinet, Netlify rebuilds the website and starts showing the new version
   to the world. This is where the "about 2 minutes" wait comes from.
   **Your website is hosted at Netlify** — not Squarespace, not Vercel, not
   GoDaddy.
3. **The editor at movewithdianag.com/admin** — the friendly front door to
   the filing cabinet, so you never look at code.
4. **Squarespace** — has only two jobs left: it rents you the name
   "movewithdianag.com", and it points that name at Netlify (while keeping
   your Gmail connected). It does **not** host your website any more.

So the whole loop is: you edit at **/admin** → the change is filed in
**GitHub** → **Netlify** notices and rebuilds → about 2 minutes later the
world sees it.

## 11. Three things not to touch

1. **The DNS settings at Squarespace.** Those records connect your domain to
   both your website *and* your Gmail. Changing them can take down your email.
2. **Anything on Squarespace's billing screens.** Two similar-looking
   subscriptions live there, and one of them — the domain — keeps your
   website AND your email alive. Don't cancel anything or turn off
   auto-renew there yourself; if a Squarespace bill looks wrong or
   confusing, ask Nick first.
3. **"Value code (internal)" boxes** in the editor. They're wiring, not words
   visitors read. Everything else is yours to change freely.

That's everything. Log in, change what you want, press Publish, give it two
minutes. You've got this.
