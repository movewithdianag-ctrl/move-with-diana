# 8-Week Pilates Program client playbook

For Diana. This is your day to day routine for the 8-Week Pilates Program, from the moment someone applies to the day they finish.

It is built to take about 15 minutes a day. Everything in a grey box is ready to copy, paste and send. Change the words in [square brackets] and make it sound like you.

Three rules sit behind every message here:

- Pilates is exercise, not medical care. Never promise to fix, heal, treat or cure anything.
- Only offer what the program includes. No discounts and no extra sessions. The program has no refund and no cancellation, so never offer either. The website says nothing about refunds or cancelling the program, and it should stay that way.
- Never put someone's health details in a text or an email subject line. Keep those for the call or the session.

## Your 15 minutes a day

1. Reply to any new application (section 1).
2. Send today's follow-up messages (section 2).
3. Send the evening-before note for tomorrow's assessments and sessions (section 3).
4. Accept or decline any booking requests waiting in Cal.com. Every booking needs your yes: the 15 minute call, the assessment and each weekly session. Nothing is confirmed until you accept it, so do not let a request sit.
5. Once a week, add the weekly check-ins (section 7) and the numbers check (section 12).

## Every Friday: plan next week

Your schedule changes every week, so you do not hold a fixed weekly time for any client. This short routine is what keeps the booking calendar true.

1. Open Apple Calendar and plan next week. Put in everything that takes your time: studio clients, appointments, family, travel.
2. That is all Cal.com needs. It offers clients Monday to Friday, 9am to 4pm Eastern, minus anything on your calendars. No Saturdays and no Sundays.
3. If you want to keep a time free without saying why, add an event called "Busy" for that time.
4. Open Cal.com and accept or decline any requests that are waiting.

Clients book their weekly sessions one at a time (section 6). The session calendar shows the next 14 days, so what you plan on Friday is what they can choose from.

## 1. The 5 minute rule

People choose the teacher who answers first. When a lead alert arrives, aim to reply within 5 minutes during your working hours. If it arrives at night, reply first thing in the morning.

**What to do when the alert arrives**

1. Read the top line of the alert email. It says one of three things: "Good fit", "Good fit, but read their notes first" or "Not cleared yet".
2. Tap **Text** if they left a mobile number. Tap **Email** if they did not, and always for "Not cleared yet". The form does not ask how they like to be contacted, so use what they gave you. If they write back on a different channel, stay on that one.
3. The first line is already typed for you ("Hi [Name], this is Diana from Move with Diana. Thank you for applying to the 8-Week Pilates Program."). Add the rest from the matching template below and send.
4. Tell them exactly when they will hear from you next.

**Good fit**

```
I read your answers and I think this program suits you well. You can reserve your place here: https://movewithdianag.com/pilates-program. If you'd like to talk first, reply here and I'll answer today. I'll check in with you again tomorrow morning.
```

If they would rather talk on video, send your 15 minute call link: https://cal.com/movewithdianag/strong-safe-call

The call is free. It is there for people who applied and still have a question. A call request is not confirmed until you accept it in Cal.com, and the client then gets the confirmation by email. Accept it quickly, because the call can be asked for as little as 2 hours ahead.

**Good fit, but read their notes first**

Read their answers in the alert email or the sheet before you write. Do not mention any health detail in the message.

```
Thank you too for telling me so much about where you are right now. I'd like to understand a little more before we begin, so I can plan your sessions with care. Could we talk for 15 minutes? You can pick a time here: https://cal.com/movewithdianag/strong-safe-call. If it's easier, reply with a good time today or tomorrow and I'll call you.
```

If you want a note from their doctor or physical therapist, ask for it on the call, not by text.

**Not cleared yet**

No payment and no booking until their doctor or PT says yes. The goal of this message is to be kind and to leave the door open.

Send this one by email only, even if they left a mobile number. It says what came out of their health answers, and that does not belong in a text.

```
From your answers, the right first step is a conversation with your doctor or physical therapist about starting exercise. Once they give you the OK, write to me and we'll pick up from here. Your place will be waiting. In the meantime you're welcome in my free community and my newsletter, The Weekly Cue.
```

Never offer a lower price or a smaller version of the program here.

## 2. Following up in the first week

For people who were approved but have not reserved. Keep it light. Each message comes from a different angle, so it never feels like the same question twice. Stop the moment they reserve or say no.

| When | Angle | Channel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 (the day after your first reply) | A question | Same as your first reply |
| Day 3 | What the first session is like | Same |
| Day 5 | A real review | Same |
| Day 7 | Closing the loop | Same |

**Day 1. A question**

```
Hi [Name], it's Diana. I was thinking about your application. What is the one thing you would most like to be able to do comfortably again? Knowing that helps me picture where we would start.
```

**Day 3. What the first session is like**

```
Hi [Name], Diana here. In case it helps to picture it: we begin with a 75 minute assessment on video. I watch how you move, I listen to your history, and we agree on what matters most to you. Nothing is rushed. If you'd like to talk it through first, here is my calendar for a 15 minute call: https://cal.com/movewithdianag/strong-safe-call
```

**Day 5. A real review**

Send the link to your Trustpilot page, so they can read your clients' own words. Never rewrite a review or make one up.

```
Hi [Name], it's Diana. If it helps to hear from people I work with, here is what my clients say: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.movewithdianag.com. If you have any question at all, just reply here.
```

**Day 7. Closing the loop**

```
Hi [Name], it's Diana. I don't want to crowd your inbox, so this is my last note for now. If the timing isn't right, that's completely fine. Whenever you're ready, reply to this message and I'll be glad to hear from you. Take good care.
```

After this, write "Closed" next to their name in the sheet and let it rest. You can add your own Notes column for this. The script leaves your own columns alone.

## 3. Before any call, assessment or session

**What happens automatically**

- Cal.com emails the client a confirmation with the video link as soon as you accept the booking. Until you accept, they only have a note saying the request was sent.
- Cal.com sends a reminder email 24 hours before and another 3 hours before every booking.
- Cal.com is also set to send a text 3 hours before, when the client typed a number into the "Phone number (Text notifications)" box on the booking form (that box is optional). We have not yet confirmed that texts really go out on the free Cal.com plan, so do not count on the text until you have tested it (see "Reminders" in `docs/SCHEDULING-SETUP.md`).

Those reminders, the Cal.com confirmation and the Stripe receipt are the only automatic messages a client gets. Everything else comes from you.

**What you add**

Short personal messages. They are the main reason people show up on time and ready. How many depends on the meeting:

- **The assessment:** all three below. It is the first meeting, so it is the one most likely to be missed.
- **A weekly session:** the evening-before note only. Send it in your daily 15 minutes.
- **A 15 minute call:** the morning note only.

**The evening before**

```
Hi [Name], it's Diana. Looking forward to seeing you tomorrow at [time]. One small thing to have ready: think of one everyday movement you'd like to feel better, like getting up from the floor or reaching a high shelf. We'll start from there.
```

**The morning of**

```
Good morning [Name]. See you at [time] today. Your video link is in the confirmation email. A mat or a carpeted floor, comfortable clothes and a glass of water are all you need.
```

**About an hour before**

```
Hi [Name], see you in about an hour. Please set your camera so I can see your whole body lying down and standing. If anything comes up, text me here.
```

**If someone misses a session**

Wait 5 minutes, then send this. Stay warm. Things happen.

```
Hi [Name], I'm on our video call now and I'll stay for another 10 minutes. I hope everything is all right. Reply here when you can.
```

A weekly session missed without 24 hours notice counts as one of their sessions (section 6). Do not promise another time in this message.

**Recording**

Sessions are on Zoom and are recorded, and the client gets the recording so they can revisit the cues at home.

- Start the recording at the beginning of every assessment and session, and say out loud that you are recording.
- Send the recording to the client afterwards, by a private link. Send it to that client only.
- Until the Zoom app is installed in your Cal.com account, booking emails carry a Cal Video link, and Cal Video does not record on the free plan. The steps to switch to Zoom are in `docs/SCHEDULING-SETUP.md`, section 5.

## 5. The first win

People stay when they feel a change early. Plan for one small, clear win at each of these points.

**By the end of the assessment, the client should**

- Feel heard. They told their story and you repeated the main points back.
- Know their starting point. One or two things you saw in how they move, said in plain words.
- Have done one movement that felt good and safe in their body.
- Know the goal you agreed for the 8 weeks, in their own words.
- Know when the next session is.

**By the end of week 2, the client should**

- Be able to do their short home practice without looking at the plan.
- Be able to name one thing that feels easier or more steady than on day one, even if it is small.

Talk about what they notice and what they can do. Do not describe it as healing or treatment.

**Small homework with a deadline**

Give one thing only, with a day attached. Send it right after the session with the written plan.

```
Hi [Name], lovely work today. Your plan is in the hub. One thing for this week: [exercise], [how many] times, on [two or three days]. Send me a quick message by [day] to tell me how it felt. See you [day] at [time].
```

## 6. Every session ends with the next one booked

The simplest way to keep someone coming is to never end a session without a date for the next one.

You do not hold the same weekly time for a client, because your schedule changes every week. Sessions are booked one at a time. There are two ways, and both are fine:

- **You agree it together.** In the last two minutes of every session, open your calendar and settle the next time out loud: "Next week I can do [day] at [time] or [day] at [time]. Which suits you?" Then put it in your calendar yourself.
- **The client books it.** They go back to their welcome page (https://movewithdianag.com/pilates-program/welcome#book-sessions) and pick a time from the weekly session calendar. It shows the next 14 days. They can come back to that page every week. You then accept the request in Cal.com and they get the confirmation by email.

The welcome page link is for program clients. For anyone else who needs to book a private session, send https://movewithdianag.com/book/session instead (see "Your three booking links" at the top).

If you end a session with a program client without a next date, send the welcome page link the same day. For anyone else, send https://movewithdianag.com/book/session.

```
Hi [Name], lovely work today. Whenever you're ready, you can book next week's session here: https://movewithdianag.com/pilates-program/welcome#book-sessions. Pick any time that suits you and I'll confirm it by email.
```

**Your 24 hour rule**

This is your policy for every session:

- A client needs to give you at least 24 hours notice to cancel or move a session.
- With less notice, the session is charged. For a program client that means it counts as one of their sessions. Add it to **Sessions used** in the sheet.
- The program itself has no refund and no cancellation.

Tell each new client the 24 hour rule yourself, at the assessment or in your first messages. The welcome page only carries one short line asking for 24 hours notice. It says nothing about charges, refunds or cancelling the program, and it should stay that way.

When someone asks to move a session with 24 hours notice or more, agree the new time in the same message. Do not leave it open.

```
Hi [Name], no problem at all, and thank you for letting me know in good time. I can do [option 1] or [option 2] instead. Which suits you?
```

When someone asks with less than 24 hours notice:

```
Hi [Name], thank you for letting me know. Because it's less than 24 hours before our session, this one counts as one of your sessions. I'm sorry we'll miss each other today. You can book your next session on your welcome page, or tell me which days suit you and I'll find a time.
```

## 7. The weekly check-in

One message per client per week, between sessions, by WhatsApp or text. It is part of what they paid for. Three parts: notice something, thank them, ask one question.

```
Hi [Name], it's Diana. I noticed how much steadier your [movement] looked on [day]. Thank you for practicing between our sessions, it shows. One question for you: how has your body felt on the days after we work together?
```

Change the question each week. A few to rotate:

- What felt easiest in your practice this week?
- Is there a movement in your day that feels different from when we started?
- Is anything in your plan unclear or hard to fit in?
- What would you like more of in our next session?

If they report sharp or unusual pain, tell them to stop that movement and to check with their doctor or PT. Then adjust the plan.

## 8. Collecting proof

Real words from real clients help the next person decide. Ask at the right moments and it never feels awkward.

**When to ask**

- Right after a clear win, when they tell you something feels better or easier. Ask if you may write it down.
- Around week 4, in the weekly check-in.
- At week 8, in the look-back session.

**Six questions to ask**

Send them as a message, or ask them out loud in week 8 and take notes.

1. What was daily life like for you before we started?
2. Is there anything you can measure that has changed? For example how long you can sit, walk or sleep comfortably, or a movement you can do now.
3. What made you hesitate before you signed up?
4. What made you decide to go ahead anyway?
5. What has changed that other people might notice, at home, at work or in the things you do?
6. How do you feel now?

Use their words exactly as they said them. Do not add numbers or claims they did not give you.

**Asking for a Trustpilot review**

```
Hi [Name], it's Diana. It has meant a lot to see how far you've come. Would you be willing to share a few words about your experience? It helps other people who are unsure whether this is for them. It takes about two minutes here: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.movewithdianag.com. Only if you're comfortable, and thank you either way.
```

When they have posted it, set **Testimonial** to Y in the sheet and send a thank-you.

**Permission to share (DRAFT, for you to approve)**

This sentence is a draft only. It is not legal advice. Read it, change it to suit you, and consider having it checked before you rely on it. Always get the answer in writing, and keep the message.

```
May I share your words on my website and social media? I would use [your first name only / your initials / no name]. If I ever want to use a photo or a short clip from one of our sessions, I will show it to you first and only use it if you say yes. You can change your mind at any time and I will take it down.
```

Never share a health detail, a photo or a recording without a clear written yes.

## 11. If someone stops or does not continue

Thank them first. Then ask these five short questions, by message or on a quick call. The answers are how the program gets better.

1. What was the main reason you decided to stop?
2. What did you find most useful?
3. What was missing, or what would you change?
4. Was there a moment when it stopped feeling right?
5. Is there anything that would make you want to come back later?

```
Hi [Name], it's Diana. Thank you for the time we worked together. I'd love to learn from your experience so I can do better for the next person. Could I ask you five short questions? You can answer in a line each, whenever you have a moment.
```

Do not argue with the answers or try to win them back in the same conversation. Thank them, and leave the door open.

## 12. The weekly numbers check (10 minutes)

Once a week, on the same day, open the Google Sheet and write down six numbers for the last 7 days. A notebook or a spare tab in the sheet is fine.

| Number | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| Applications | New rows this week |
| Approved | Rows where **Status** is QUALIFIED |
| Reserved | Rows where **Paid?** is Y |
| Booked | Rows with a **Start date** filled in |
| Showed up | Rows where **Sessions used** is 1 or more |
| Reviews collected | Rows where **Testimonial** is Y |

You fill in Paid?, Start date, Sessions used and Testimonial by hand, so keep them up to date as part of your daily 15 minutes. A session cancelled or moved with less than 24 hours notice counts in Sessions used (section 6).

**What the numbers tell you**

- Many applications, few approved: the wrong people are finding the form. Look at where they come from (the Source column).
- Many approved, few reserved: reply faster and follow section 2 every time.
- Reserved but not booked: send the day one hello sooner, with the welcome page link.
- Booked but not showing up: do not skip the three personal messages in section 3.
- Few reviews: ask at week 4 and week 8, every client, every time.

**About the price**

The price is your decision and it never changes by itself. A sensible time to think about raising it is after a steady run of sales at the current price, when your available hours are filling and people are reserving without hesitation. If you do raise it, do it in small steps, and never for someone who has already been quoted the current price. To change it, the price lives in the website editor and in your Stripe payment link, and both must match (see `docs/STRIPE-SETUP.md`).
